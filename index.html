Overview

Research

My research programme is unified by a single underlying concern: how people build and sustain community through their everyday digital engagements, and how that understanding can be used to make participation in digital spaces safer and fairer — particularly for marginalised groups who are often at greater risk of harm.

This vision connects two strands that run through my work. The first, rooted in my doctoral research, explores how people cultivate skill, belonging, and wellbeing in online communities, drawing on Material Engagement Theory and digital sensory ethnographic methods. The second, developed through my postdoctoral work, examines how the design and governance of digital platforms shapes and constrains that participation — through analysis of platform policy documents, co-design with practitioners, and critical engagement with how harm is constructed and governed.

I hold concurrent 0.5 FTE Senior Research Fellow positions at UCL's Department of Computer Science (Information Security Group) and KCL's Department of Informatics, and am an affiliated member of UCL's Gender and Tech Research Group.

Current & Recent

Projects

Google Gift Grant · UCL & UCLIC

Reimagining Privacy Harm Reporting Using Conversational User Interfaces

Exploring how conversational AI and large language models might support the reporting of complex privacy harms, through expert design workshops with practitioners in policing, mental health, restorative justice, and VAWG support. Named Research Fellow.

EPSRC · UCL & KCL · In preparation

The Online Platform Governance Observatory

A longitudinal infrastructure to monitor and analyse how platform governance shifts over time. Work package lead for the co-design of a public-facing interactive visualisation dashboard. £2 million proposal.

REPHRAIN · UCL

UseCRT: Usability of Online Content Reporting Tools

Systematic analysis of 517 policy-related documents across 25 platforms in four categories, examining how platforms construct and govern harassment. Led to CHI 2025 publication and invited engagement with Ofcom and REPHRAIN.

KCL · US Collaboration

Scammed by Love: AI-Facilitated Romance Fraud

Cross-national study of AI-facilitated romance scams targeting older adults, combining focus groups, a 13-country survey, and community workshops. Collaborating with University of Maryland and George Mason University.

UKRI · UCL

Digital Forensic Triage in Policing

Examining how frontline officers triage digital evidence at scene, using co-design and ethnographic methods. Findings have directly informed redesigned police training recognised by the College of Policing.
